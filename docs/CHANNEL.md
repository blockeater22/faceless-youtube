# Channel brand kit

Everything you need to set up the YouTube channel. Assets are generated by
`branding/make_brand.py` — edit `NAME`/`TAGLINE`/colours there and re-run to
rebrand.

---

## Channel name

### ⭐ Recommended: **Inklings**
- *Inkling* = a small idea, a hint, a suspicion — exactly what these stories
  plant. It also literally means "little ink (drawings)." Double meaning, warm,
  short, brandable, and **broad enough to tell any story** (not boxed into one
  genre).
- **Tagline:** *little drawings · big what-ifs*
- **Handle (pick one that's free):** `@inklings.tv` · `@theinklings` ·
  `@inklingstories` · `@inklingsdoodle`
  ⚠️ I can't check handle/name availability from here — search YouTube and grab
  the closest free handle. The *display name* "Inklings" can repeat across
  channels; only the @handle must be unique.

## Profile picture — `branding/profile.png` (1024×1024)
Icon-only (a figure mid-wonder + the signature **?**) so it stays legible when
YouTube shrinks it to a circle. The accent ring sits inside the safe crop. Works
for any of the names above (no text baked in).

## Banner — `branding/banner.png` (2560×1440)
Wordmark + tagline centred in the device-safe area, with a faint stick-figure
crowd. Re-run `make_brand.py` after changing `NAME`/`TAGLINE`.

> Upload both in **YouTube Studio → Customisation → Branding**. Recommended
> banner safe area is the central 1546×423 — the text is already inside it.

## Video watermark — `branding/watermark.png` (800×800, transparent)
The clickable **subscribe watermark** shown in the corner of every video. It's
the figure-in-ring "badge" (a simplified profile pic) with a soft dark halo so it
reads on any footage — light or dark.

- **Where:** YouTube Studio → Customisation → **Branding → Video watermark**.
- **Specs (met):** transparent PNG, 1:1 square, ≥150×150, <1 MB.
- **Display time:** choose *Entire video* so it's always available to tap.

> This is separate from the small `@handle` text currently burned into the top of
> each rendered video (set via the story's `handle` field). Want the badge burned
> into the corner of the videos themselves instead? That's a quick engine tweak.

## Content pillars (keep the channel coherent)
1. **What-ifs** — speculative hooks ("what if you could see everyone's lifespan?").
2. **Parallel lives** — the road-not-taken, other versions of you.
3. **Emotional micro-stories** — a twist or gut-punch in under a minute.
4. **Quiet philosophy** — a single idea, beautifully simple.

**Cadence:** batch-write 8–10 scripts, render overnight, post 3–5 Shorts/week;
compile the best 6–8 into a monthly 16:9 long-form for watch-time + ad revenue.

**Thumbnail formula (for long-form):** one bold stick figure + the signature
amber **?** or a single accent prop, big 3–5 word question in white. Keep it the
same every time so the channel is recognisable in the feed.
