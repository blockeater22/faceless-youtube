## 1-month engagement playbook (do this, not "post and pray")

**Cadence:** 3 shorts/week (Mon/Wed/Fri), one at a time. Quality + consistency
beats volume — the old 36/week mill is what buried the channel.

**The first 60 minutes decide everything.** When you post:
1. **Pin the question** as the first comment (it's already the last line of each
   video). A pinned question gets 5–10× the replies of an unpinned one.
2. **Reply to every comment** for the first hour, and ask a follow-up question
   back ("how long did yours last?"). Each reply is another engagement signal +
   it pulls the commenter back for a notification.
3. **Like every comment.** Costs nothing, doubles the chance they comment again.

**Posting window:** 8:00 AM or 8:00 PM in your audience's timezone (these
reflective topics over-index at night). Use Studio → Schedule.

**Watch this metric, not subs:** YouTube Studio → Analytics → "Stayed to watch"
(retention) and "Viewed vs swiped away". Sub growth is a lagging result of those.

**Comment-bait line to pin (per video):** see each entry below.

  suggested) and **names the psychology term** for SEO discovery.

  biggest lever for comments on a small channel.

**Title:**

```
Why You Can't Stop Thinking About Unfinished Tasks
```

**Description:**
```

That nagging isn't failure — it's a built-in reminder system. Write the task down and the loop goes quiet; your brain trusts paper more than memory. One small step closes the loop your mind keeps reopening.
```
